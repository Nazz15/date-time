Accurate Sun & Moon Information

TimezoneBudy gives you reliable sunrise, sunset and moon data for any city in the world, on any date. Plan your day, track moon phases and explore daylight hours — all calculated with astronomical formulas, so there's no API key or sign-up required.

For each location you can see the full sun journey — dawn, sunrise, solar noon, sunset and dusk — along with day length and remaining daylight. The moon section shows the current phase, illumination percentage, moonrise and moonset, and the dates of the next full moon and new moon. Change the date to see how these values shift throughout the month.

Compare sunrise, sunset and moon phases across major cities, and browse the upcoming moon phase calendar to plan around natural light — useful for photographers, travelers, gardeners and anyone who follows the sky.
