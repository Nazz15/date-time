Quick answers

The questions people ask us most.

What time is it in UTC right now?

It's --:-- UTC. UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the world's reference time. It never changes for daylight saving, so it is the same everywhere. Every time zone is described as an offset from it, such as UTC+5:30 for India or UTC-5 for New York in winter.

How do I convert time between two time zones?

Pick a "From" city, enter the time, and pick a "To" city in the converter at the top of this page. The result updates instantly and accounts for daylight saving time on that date. For more than two cities, open the full Time Zone Converter.

Does TimezoneBudy adjust for daylight saving time?

Yes. All times use your browser's built-in IANA time zone database, so daylight saving changes are applied automatically for every city, including countries that change on different dates, like the US and the UK.

How do I find the best meeting time across time zones?

Add each participant's city to the Meeting Planner. It lines up 9am–6pm working hours for every city and highlights the hours where they overlap, so you can pick a slot that works for everyone.
