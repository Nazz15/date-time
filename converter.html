How to use the Meeting Planner

  1. Add every city your attendees are in. Star ☆ the city you want hours shown in.
  2. Pick the meeting date: daylight saving changes are handled for that exact day.
  3. Look for green in the overlap bar, or pick one of the suggested best times.
  4. Click a slot, then copy the times, add it to your calendar or share the link.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a good meeting time across time zones?

Add the city of every attendee, pick the meeting date and look at the overlap bar under the timeline. Green hours are inside everyone’s work hours; amber hours keep most people working with nobody asleep. The “Best times” cards rank the top three slots for you.

Does the Meeting Planner handle daylight saving time?

Yes. Times are calculated for the exact date you choose, so if London or New York changes its clocks that week, the timeline and suggestions shift automatically.

Can I share the meeting time with my team?

Yes. Select a slot and use Share link: anyone who opens it sees the same cities, date and time. You can also copy all local times as text or add the meeting to Google Calendar, Outlook or any calendar app (.ics file).

Can I convert one specific time between cities?

Yes. In the selected-slot bar, type a time and choose the city it belongs to: every other city updates instantly. You can also click any hour on the timeline.

Can I change the work hours?

Use the Work hours menu at the top to pick 8–16, 9–17, 9–18, 10–18 or 10–19. The colours, overlap bar and suggestions all follow your choice.

Are my cities saved?

Yes. Your cities, meeting length and work hours are saved in your browser, and cities you star in World Clock or World Map appear as one-tap “Saved” chips.
