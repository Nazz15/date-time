When Do the Clocks Change? Daylight Saving Time 2027 Dates

Every 2027 clock change for the US, Canada, UK, Europe, Australia and New Zealand, plus who doesn't change at all.

Key takeaways
  • US & Canada: forward 14 Mar 2027, back 7 Nov 2027 (2:00 AM local).
  • UK & EU: forward 28 Mar 2027, back 31 Oct 2027 (01:00 UTC).
  • India, China, Japan, the UAE and most of Africa don't change their clocks.

Quick answer: in 2027, clocks go forward on Sunday 14 March in the US and Canada and on Sunday 28 March in the UK and EU. They go back on Sunday 31 October in the UK and EU and Sunday 7 November in the US and Canada. In Australia and New Zealand it works the other way round.

What daylight saving time actually does

Daylight saving time (DST), called "summer time" in the UK and Europe, moves clocks one hour forward in spring and one hour back in autumn. The idea is to shift daylight from early morning, when most people are asleep, into the evening.

The easy way to remember it is spring forward, fall back. In March you lose an hour of sleep; in October or November you get it back. The change happens overnight at a weekend to cause the least disruption.

Roughly a third of countries still use it, mostly in North America, Europe and parts of the Southern Hemisphere. Most of Asia, Africa and South America don't.

Europe changes at the same moment everywhere (01:00 UTC), so the local clock time differs by country.

For reference, the most recent changes were on 25 October 2026 in the UK and EU and 1 November 2026 in the US, so we're all on standard (winter) time right now in the Northern Hemisphere.

The rules behind the dates

The dates move every year, but the rules don't, so you can work them out yourself:

  • US and Canada: second Sunday in March to first Sunday in November.
  • UK and EU: last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October.
  • Australia: first Sunday in October to first Sunday in April.
  • New Zealand: last Sunday in September to first Sunday in April.

Who doesn't change their clocks

Many places stay on the same time all year. If you're converting to any of these, the offset never changes on their side, but the gap with DST countries does.

  • Asia: India, China, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, the UAE, Saudi Arabia and most other Asian countries.
  • United States: most of Arizona (except the Navajo Nation) and Hawaii, plus US territories like Puerto Rico.
  • Australia: Queensland, Western Australia and the Northern Territory.
  • The Americas: Mexico dropped DST in 2022 (apart from some border areas), and Brazil stopped in 2019.
  • Africa: almost the whole continent.

This is why the time difference between New York and Mumbai changes from 10.5 to 9.5 hours in March. India didn't move; New York did. See more examples in time differences between major cities.

The "gap weeks" that confuse international calls

Because the US and Europe switch on different weekends, there are two short windows each year when the usual time differences are wrong by an hour:

  • 14–28 March 2027: New York is 4 hours behind London (not 5), and Los Angeles is 7 hours behind (not 8).
  • 31 October–7 November 2027: the same 4-hour and 7-hour gaps apply again.

If you run a weekly call between the US and Europe, this is the week someone turns up an hour early. Calendar apps handle it correctly, but anything written in an email ("see you at 3 PM my time, 10 AM yours") won't. 💡 Tip: on a spring-forward night, 2:00–2:59 AM doesn't exist in the US. Anything scheduled in that hour (backups, alarms, automated emails) can run late or not at all. In autumn, 1:00–1:59 AM happens twice.

Is daylight saving time being abolished?

It comes up every year. In the US, the Sunshine Protection Act, which would make daylight saving time permanent, has been introduced in Congress several times. In Europe, the European Parliament voted in 2019 to end seasonal clock changes, but member states never agreed on how to do it. Clock changes are still scheduled in both regions for 2027, and we'll update this page if that changes.

Frequently asked questions

Do we lose or gain an hour in March?

In the Northern Hemisphere you lose an hour in March: clocks spring forward from 2:00 AM to 3:00 AM (1:00 AM to 2:00 AM in the UK). You get it back in autumn when the clocks fall back.

When does daylight saving time end in 2027?

In the US and Canada, DST ends on Sunday 7 November 2027 at 2:00 AM. In the UK and EU, summer time ends on Sunday 31 October 2027. Australia's daylight saving ends on Sunday 4 April 2027.

Does India observe daylight saving time?

No. India stays on IST (UTC+5:30) all year. That's why the time difference between India and the US or UK changes by an hour twice a year, even though Indian clocks never move.

Will my phone change the time automatically?

Yes, if automatic date and time is switched on. Phones, laptops and most smartwatches update overnight. Older ovens, car dashboards and some wall clocks need changing by hand.

Why is the US–UK time difference only 4 hours in March?

Because the US moves its clocks forward two weeks before the UK. Between 14 and 28 March 2027, New York is on EDT while London is still on GMT, so the gap drops from 5 hours to 4. The same happens between 31 October and 7 November.
