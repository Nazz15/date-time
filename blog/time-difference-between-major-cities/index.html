Time Difference Between New York, London, Dubai, India and Other Major Cities

Live and seasonal time differences for the city pairs people search for most, with the best hours to call each one.

Key takeaways
  • New York → London: 5 hours almost all year (4 hours in two short gap periods).
  • India and Dubai don't change clocks, so their gap with the US and UK shifts by 1 hour in March and Oct/Nov.
  • Dubai is always 1 hour 30 minutes behind India.

Quick answer: London is 5 hours ahead of New York. Dubai is 4 hours ahead of London in winter (3 in summer). India is 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of New York in winter (9.5 in summer) and 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of London in winter (4.5 in summer). Dubai is always 1.5 hours behind India.

Why some differences change during the year

A time difference only stays fixed if both cities follow the same daylight saving rules, or neither does. New York and London both use DST, so their gap stays at 5 hours most of the time. India, Dubai and Singapore don't use DST at all, so when the US or UK moves its clocks, the gap with them moves by an hour.

Sydney is the trickiest. Australia is in the Southern Hemisphere, so it moves its clocks in the opposite direction. When London goes forward in March, Sydney goes back in April, and the gap swings by two hours across the year. The exact dates are in our 2027 clock change calendar.

The city pairs people ask about most

New York and London

London is 5 hours ahead, so 9:00 AM in New York is 2:00 PM in London. Their working days overlap from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM New York time (2:00–5:00 PM London), which is the sweet spot for calls. Watch out for 14–28 March and 31 October–7 November 2027, when the gap is only 4 hours.

London and Dubai

Dubai is 4 hours ahead in winter and 3 in summer. London mornings work well: 9:00 AM in London is 1:00 PM in Dubai in winter. One thing that catches people out is the weekend. The UAE moved to a Saturday–Sunday weekend in 2022, but in neighbouring Saudi Arabia the weekend is still Friday–Saturday.

India and the USA

This is the one I deal with most. India is 10.5 hours ahead of New York in winter and 9.5 in summer, which leaves almost no overlap in normal office hours. The usual compromise is the Indian evening and the US morning: 8:00 PM in Mumbai is 9:30 AM in New York in winter. With the West Coast it's even tighter. India is 13.5 hours ahead of Los Angeles in winter, so calls usually happen in the US evening and the Indian morning. Our PST to IST conversion chart lays out the hour-by-hour options.

India and the UK

Much easier. With a 5.5-hour gap in winter (4.5 in summer), the UK morning lines up with the Indian afternoon. 10:00 AM in London is 3:30 PM in India in winter and 2:30 PM in summer.

London and Sydney

Sydney is 9 to 11 hours ahead, depending on the time of year. The workable slots are early morning in London (evening in Sydney) or late evening in London (next morning in Sydney).

Frequently asked questions

Is India 9.5 or 10.5 hours ahead of New York?

Both, depending on the season. India is 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of New York from early November to mid-March (when New York is on EST) and 9 hours 30 minutes ahead from mid-March to early November (EDT).

How many hours ahead is Dubai from London?

Dubai is 4 hours ahead of London in winter (GMT) and 3 hours ahead in summer (BST). Dubai stays on UTC+4 all year.

Is London always 5 hours ahead of New York?

Almost. The gap is 5 hours for most of the year, but it's 4 hours for about two weeks in March and one week around the start of November, because the US and UK change their clocks on different dates.

What time is it in Dubai when it's 9 AM in London?

1:00 PM in winter and 12:00 PM in summer.

What is the best time to call the USA from India?

Between 7:30 PM and 10:30 PM India time, which is 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM in New York in winter. In US summer, the same New York hours fall between 6:30 PM and 9:30 PM in India.
