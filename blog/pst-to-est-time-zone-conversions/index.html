PST to EST, CST, GMT and IST: Time Zone Conversions Made Simple

The differences you actually need, conversion charts you can scan in seconds, and when 'PST' quietly becomes PDT.

NNazz · SEO & time-zone nerd· Updated 26 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways
  • PST → EST: add 3 hours. PST → CST: add 2. These never change during the year.
  • PST → GMT is +8 hours and PST → IST is +13:30 in winter (+12:30 in US summer).
  • From 14 Mar to 7 Nov 2027, Pacific Time is PDT, not PST. Many people still write PST.

Quick answer: PST is 3 hours behind EST, 2 hours behind CST, 1 hour behind MST, 8 hours behind GMT and 13 hours 30 minutes behind IST. So 9:00 AM PST is 12:00 PM EST, 11:00 AM CST, 5:00 PM GMT and 10:30 PM IST.

US and international abbreviations explained

Abbreviations are handy shorthand, but they hide the daylight saving switch. Here's what each one actually means.

MST / MDTMountain Standard / Daylight Time−7 / −6Denver, Calgary (Phoenix stays on MST)
CST / CDTCentral Standard / Daylight Time−6 / −5Chicago, Dallas, Houston

GMT / BSTGreenwich Mean / British Summer Time+0 / +1London, Edinburgh

Two quirks to know. First, most of Arizona doesn't observe daylight saving, so in summer Phoenix matches Los Angeles instead of Denver. Second, "IST" also stands for Irish and Israel Standard Time, so when you're writing to people outside India, "India time" is clearer. PST vs PDT: which one is it right now?

Technically, PST only applies in winter. From 14 March to 7 November 2027, the West Coast is on PDT (UTC−7). In practice, lots of people write "PST" all year, and nobody will be confused as long as you're talking about Pacific local time.

Where it does matter is when you convert to somewhere that doesn't change its clocks, like India, Dubai or Singapore, or to the UK during the gap weeks. If you're unsure, write "PT" or "Los Angeles time". The exact switch dates are in our daylight saving time 2027 guide.

💡 Tip: scheduling across the US and India? India is 9.5 hours ahead of New York in summer and 10.5 in winter. Recurring calls set in Indian time will move by an hour in US calendars twice a year.

Frequently asked questions

Is PST 3 hours behind EST?

Yes. Pacific Time is always 3 hours behind Eastern Time, because both switch to daylight saving on the same dates. 9:00 AM in Los Angeles is 12:00 PM in New York, in winter and summer.

Is it PST or PDT right now?

Pacific Time uses PDT (UTC−7) from the second Sunday in March to the first Sunday in November, and PST (UTC−8) the rest of the year. In 2027 that means PDT runs from 14 March to 7 November. If you're unsure, write 'PT' (Pacific Time), which is correct all year.

What is 12 PM PST in IST?

12:00 PM PST is 1:30 AM the next day in India. During US daylight saving (PDT) it's 12:30 AM the next day, because India doesn't change its clocks.

What is UTC to EST?

EST is UTC−5, so subtract 5 hours: 15:00 UTC is 10:00 AM EST. During daylight saving, Eastern Time is EDT (UTC−4), so subtract 4 hours instead.

How far ahead is GMT from EST?

GMT is 5 hours ahead of EST. In summer the UK uses BST and New York uses EDT, which is still a 5-hour gap. It drops to 4 hours for a few weeks in March and around the start of November, when only one side has changed its clocks.
