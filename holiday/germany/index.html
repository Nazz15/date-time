Germany holiday questions

Quick answers about holidays in Germany.

Data: official holiday lists via the open-source holidays dataset, cross-checked with workalendar; cultural observances curated by TimezoneBuddy. Last updated 10 November 2026. Spotted an error? Let us know.

How many public holidays are there in Germany in 2026?

There are 9 nationwide public holidays in Germany in 2026 and 9 in 2027, plus additional holidays that apply only in some states. Turn on the other categories above to also see religious and cultural festivals and observances.

What are the public holidays in Germany in 2027?

New Year's Day (1 Jan), Good Friday (26 Mar), Easter Monday (29 Mar), Labor Day (1 May), Ascension Day (6 May), Pentecost Monday (17 May), German Unity Day (3 Oct), Christmas Day (25 Dec), Second Day of Christmas (26 Dec).

Are holidays different in each state?

Yes. Some holidays only apply in certain states. Use the State selector at the top of the page to see the complete calendar for one state, or choose “Nationwide only”.

Why are some dates marked “estimated”?

Holidays based on the lunar calendar, such as Eid and other Islamic holidays, depend on moon sighting and are confirmed by the authorities shortly beforehand. We show the expected date and update it when the official date is announced.

Where does this holiday data come from?

Official holidays come from the open-source holidays dataset, which follows government announcements, and are cross-checked against a second independent dataset. Cultural observances are added by our team. Last updated 10 November 2026.
