Quick answers about holidays in Japan.

How many public holidays are there in Japan in 2026?

There are 18 nationwide public holidays in Japan in 2026 and 17 in 2027. Turn on the other categories above to also see religious and cultural festivals and observances.

What are the public holidays in Japan in 2027?

New Year's Day (1 Jan), Coming of Age Day (11 Jan), Foundation Day (11 Feb), Emperor's Birthday (23 Feb), Vernal Equinox Day (21 Mar), Substitute Holiday (22 Mar), Showa Day (29 Apr), Constitution Day (3 May), Greenery Day (4 May), Children's Day (5 May), Marine Day (19 Jul), Mountain Day (11 Aug), Respect for the Aged Day (20 Sep), Autumnal Equinox Day (23 Sep), Sports Day (11 Oct), Culture Day (3 Nov), Labor Thanksgiving Day (23 Nov).

Why are some dates marked “estimated”?

Holidays based on the lunar calendar, such as Eid and other Islamic holidays, depend on moon sighting and are confirmed by the authorities shortly beforehand. We show the expected date and update it when the official date is announced.

Where does this holiday data come from?

Official holidays come from the open-source holidays dataset, which follows government announcements, and are cross-checked against a second independent dataset. Cultural observances are added by our team. Last updated 10 November 2026.
