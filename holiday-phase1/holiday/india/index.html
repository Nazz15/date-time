India holiday questions

Quick answers about holidays in India.

Data: official holiday lists via the open-source holidays dataset, cross-checked with workalendar; cultural observances curated by TimezoneBuddy. Last updated 10 November 2026. Spotted an error? Let us know.

How many public holidays are there in India in 2026?

There are 18 nationwide public holidays in India in 2026 and 19 in 2027, plus additional holidays that apply only in some states. Turn on the other categories above to also see religious and cultural festivals and observances.

What are the public holidays in India in 2027?

Republic Day (26 Jan), Maha Shivaratri (6 Mar), Id-ul-Fitr (10 Mar), Holi (22 Mar), Good Friday (26 Mar), Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's Jayanti (14 Apr), Ram Navami (15 Apr), Mahavir Jayanti (18 Apr), Id-ul-Zuha (Bakrid) (17 May), Buddha Purnima (20 May), Muharram (16 Jun), Independence Day (15 Aug), Milad-un-Nabi (15 Aug), Janmashtami (Vaishnava) (25 Aug), Mahatma Gandhi's Jayanti (2 Oct), Dussehra (9 Oct), Diwali (Deepavali) (29 Oct), Guru Nanak's Jayanti (14 Nov), Christmas (25 Dec).

Are holidays different in each state?

Yes. Some holidays only apply in certain states. Use the State selector at the top of the page to see the complete calendar for one state, or choose “Nationwide only”.

What is a restricted holiday in India?

Restricted (optional) holidays are days that central government employees may choose to take from a published list, up to a limit each year. They are shown here as Observance with the note “Restricted / optional holiday”; gazetted holidays are shown as Public.

Why are some dates marked “estimated”?

Holidays based on the lunar calendar, such as Eid and other Islamic holidays, depend on moon sighting and are confirmed by the authorities shortly beforehand. We show the expected date and update it when the official date is announced.

Where does this holiday data come from?

Official holidays come from the open-source holidays dataset, which follows government announcements, and are cross-checked against a second independent dataset. Cultural observances are added by our team. Last updated 10 November 2026.
