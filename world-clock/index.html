World clock questions

Our World Clock shows the current time, date and time zone information for cities around the world. All times update live and automatically handle Daylight Saving Time and UTC offsets using the IANA time zone database.

How do I add or remove cities?

Search for any city, country or time zone in the "Add a city" box at the top, or tap + on any popular city. To remove a city, use the × button on its row (on mobile, tap the row or swipe it left). You can also drag cities to reorder them.

Are my cities saved?

Yes. Your cities are saved in your browser on this device, so they're still there when you come back. They're shared with the "Your cities" strip on the homepage. Nothing is stored on our servers.

Does the world clock handle daylight saving time?

Yes. Every clock uses the IANA time zone database built into your browser, so daylight saving changes are applied automatically on the right date for every city.

What does the time travel slider do?

Drag it to move every clock forward or back by up to 12 hours at once. It's the quickest way to answer "if it's 4pm here, what time is it for everyone else?". Tap "Reset to now" to go back to live time.

Can I share my world clock with someone?

Yes. Tap Share to copy a link with your cities. Whoever opens it sees your list and can add those cities to their own world clock in one tap.
