Free Weather Forecast for Any City

TimezoneBudy provides accurate and up-to-date weather information for cities around the world. Whether you're planning a trip, checking the weather at home, or just curious about conditions in another city, our tool gives you the current weather, hourly forecast, and 7-day forecast in an easy-to-use format.

You can search for any city or country to get detailed weather information including temperature, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind speed, visibility, sunrise and sunset times, and more. All data is powered by Open-Meteo — a free, open-source weather API with no registration required.
