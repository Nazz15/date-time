Frequently asked questions

How many time zones are there in the world?

In theory there are 24 one-hour zones, but around 38 different UTC offsets are in use today, from UTC−12:00 to UTC+14:00. Some places, such as India (UTC+05:30) and Nepal (UTC+05:45), use half-hour or 45-minute offsets.

Which countries have more than one time zone?

Large countries often span several zones. In this directory the United States and Canada each use six main time zones, Mexico and Brazil four, Australia and Indonesia three, and Spain two (mainland and the Canary Islands). China and India use a single time zone despite their size.

What is the difference between GMT and UTC?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the global time standard that every time zone is measured from. GMT (Greenwich Mean Time) is the time zone used by the UK in winter, and it matches UTC+00:00. In summer the UK switches to BST (UTC+01:00).

Do these times adjust for daylight saving time?

Yes. Every clock on TimezoneBuddy uses your browser’s up-to-date time zone database, so offsets and abbreviations switch automatically when a country starts or ends daylight saving time.

How do I find the time difference between two cities?

Open any city page to see its difference from London, New York and Tokyo, or use the Time Difference Calculator to compare any two cities and find good meeting times.
