Time difference questions

Times come from the IANA time zone database built into your browser, so daylight saving changes are always applied — for every city, on the right date.

How do I calculate the time difference between two places?

Pick a city, country or time zone in the From and To fields. The calculator shows both local times, the exact difference in hours and minutes, and which place is ahead or behind. It updates instantly and accounts for daylight saving time.

Does the time difference change with daylight saving time?

Yes. When one place moves its clocks and the other doesn't, or they change on different dates, the gap between them changes. This calculator checks the next 12 months and tells you the date the difference will change.

Why is the other city on a different day?

When two places are far apart, one local clock can pass midnight while it is still the previous day in the other. The date under each clock and the next day or previous day badge make this clear.

What is the best time to call someone in another time zone?

Look for hours where both places are within normal working hours, 9am to 6pm. The hour-by-hour comparison on this page highlights those shared hours and shows them in your local time.

Can I compare a country instead of a city?

Yes. Search by country and the calculator uses a representative time zone for it. For countries with several time zones, such as the United States or Australia, pick a specific city for the most precise result.
