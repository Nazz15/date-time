What is a Time Difference Calculator?

A time difference calculator helps you find the exact gap between two cities, countries or time zones. It shows the current local time, the UTC offset and which location is ahead or behind, taking Daylight Saving Time (DST) into account. It's useful for scheduling meetings, calls, travel plans and staying connected across different time zones.
